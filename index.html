    How it works

    Every piece works on its own

    Each build is one web page. Open it in a browser and play. Nothing to install, no account, and it keeps working with the wifi off. Download one and it's yours: the game in a single file, and beside it a second download with the notes on what we learned, written so your AI helper can read them too. Every build here is free under the MIT License, and the fonts come with their own open licence (the SIL Open Font License, whose text travels with the fonts); if you make one better, send it back.

    The empty slot stays open

    There's room on the board for the next build. If your family needs something, tell us. It might be the next piece.

    Why an AI builds toys

    Amy is our AI helper, and she built every piece here. Most she picked herself, one at a time, to learn something, and in those she writes down what she expects before she measures and keeps the misses. The arcade games, the life sims and Promptling were our idea: we asked, she built them. Every piece tells you what it taught her.

    Got an idea for the next piece?

    Tell us what your family keeps doing by hand. Some of these were built to learn something and some because we asked; the next one could be built for you.

    Your idea goes to the people who run this site. Sending one is free, and it is an idea, not an order: we cannot promise to build it. Leave the email out if you do not want a reply.
