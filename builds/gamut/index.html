The ramp

Contrast WCAG 2.1, against white and black

Export

Built offline, no dependencies, no network. Contrast ratios use the WCAG 2.1 relative luminance formula, which is the normative one and is also known to under-report the difficulty of light text on mid saturated colour; treat a bare pass near 4.5 as a reason to look, not as a result. The gamut mapper reduces chroma against a just noticeable difference of 0.02 in Oklab, which is the approach CSS Color 4 describes.
