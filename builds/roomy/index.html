Roomy

A cosy room decorating puzzle. Twelve rooms, each with a brief and a tray of furniture. Make the room work and it earns its stars.

  • Drag a piece from the tray into the room, or tap it and then tap a spot. R or Rotate turns it; a swatch repaints it.
  • The judge checks six things: the brief, a clear walkway from the door to every piece, seats facing a table or TV, light reaching every seat, accent colours from one family, and nothing blocking the door or a window.
  • One star unlocks the next room. Three stars is a perfect 100.
  • Keys: N / B pick a piece, arrows move, Enter puts down or picks up, R rotate, C colour, Del to tray, Z undo, L rooms, M sound.
