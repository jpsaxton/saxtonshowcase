    Hearsay Lane

    A little street where everybody knows everybody's business.
    • Days pass on their own. Neighbours visit, meet in the park and at the shop, and grow closer or grumpier.
    • Watch the lines: green friends, red rivals, pink romance. Thicker means stronger.
    • Nudge things along: throw a party, introduce two people, start a rumour (careful, it can backfire), or plant a garden.
    • Tap anyone to see who they like, rename them or change their leanings.
    Keys: Space pause, 1 2 3 speed, P party, I introduce, R rumour, G garden, E people, [ ] step through people, S street menu, M mute.
