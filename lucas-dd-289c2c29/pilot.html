Tell us how it went

For families in the pilot, after the request has gone in. A few questions about how it went. The page makes a short record you can send to the pilot partner, if you choose.

This is a family's own planning tool. It is not the Lucas County Board of Developmental Disabilities, is not connected to it, and does not decide eligibility. Only the board's trained staff decide.

Before you start

  • It helps to have nearby: the dates you asked, when Lucas DD had your papers, and when you got the decision.
  • The record has no place for a name, birth date, address, diagnosis or health detail. Please keep the notes that way too.
  • Phone numbers and email addresses typed in a note are removed before the record is made.
  • You can skip anything, or choose unsure.

Nothing you type leaves this device. The page saves nothing. Close it and it is gone.

Each question has a short note under it that says what it means. Read the full guide for this page, or find the right page for you.
