    Getting help from Lucas County DD, step by step

    Which page do I need?

    Pick the sentence that sounds most like you.

    • "I want to find out if I, or my child, can get services from Lucas DD." This is the place to start if you are new.
      Open the front door page What it asks
    • "My child is younger than 3 and may be behind." Start with the front door page. It will point you to Help Me Grow, for Early Intervention.
      Open the front door page
    • "We already get services from Lucas DD. We need help paying for something, like respite care or equipment."
      Open the Family Support Services page What it asks
    • "We need more help at home. We want a Medicaid waiver, or a place on the waiting list."
      Open the waiting list page What it asks
    • "Lucas DD turned down a request, or cut, paused or stopped a service. Or we have a problem with Lucas DD."
      Open the appeal and complaint page What it asks
    • "We are in the pilot. We want to tell how it went."
      Open the pilot record page What it asks

    Good to know first

    • Nothing you type leaves your phone or computer. The pages have no account and save nothing. When you close a page, your answers are gone.
    • You can skip questions. If you do not know, choose unsure. That is always fine.
    • Each question has a short note under it that says what it means.
    • The pages never decide anything. Only Lucas DD staff decide.
    • The pages never send anything. You send things yourself, when you are ready.
    • Every page has worked examples near the top. Try one first to see how it works.
    • Save or copy your results before you close the page.

    Can I upload papers or connect an account?

    No. This is on purpose. The pages cannot upload files. They do not connect to Lucas DD or to any account. This keeps your information on your own device.

    Here is what you can do instead:

    • If the page shows a Save button, use it to save your results as a file. Then attach that file to an email you send yourself.
    • Use the Copy button, and paste the text into an email or a letter you send.
    • Take a photo of a paper with your phone camera, so you have it with you when you call.
    • Lucas DD Intake has an email address: Intake@lucasdd.org. We do not know yet if Intake takes papers by email. Ask when you call.

    Ways to make the pages easier to use

    You can use the tools already on your phone or computer. Nothing extra is needed.

    • Bigger text. Pinch the screen, or use your browser's zoom. The pages grow to fit. On iPhone, Zoom makes the whole screen bigger.
    • Hear the page read out loud. Turn on your phone's screen reader. On Android it is called TalkBack: open Settings, tap Accessibility, then TalkBack, then Use TalkBack. On iPhone it is called VoiceOver, and Speak Screen reads the whole screen out loud. Apple's accessibility page describes them.
    • Braille. VoiceOver can show what is on the screen in braille, on a braille display.
    • A keyboard instead of touch. Press Tab to move to the next question. Use the arrow keys to pick a choice. Press Enter on a button. Each page starts with a link that skips to the main part of the page.
    • Light screen. The pages stay light, even when your phone or computer is set to dark mode. If the screen feels too bright, turn down its brightness.
    • Someone beside you. A family member, a friend or an advocate can fill in a page with you.
    • Phone calls. If you are deaf, hard of hearing, or have trouble speaking, dial 711 to use Ohio Relay (run by Hamilton Relay) for your calls to Lucas DD.

    These pages aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the standard the Justice Department's ADA rule sets for state and local government web pages. They were checked by tests, not yet by a person using a screen reader. If something does not work for you, please tell whoever gave you these pages.

    Front door: ask if a person can get services

    Use this page to ask Lucas DD if a person can get its services. It shows which office to call, lists the papers to gather, works out the dates, and writes a message you can read on the phone or send.

    Open this page

    Before you start

    • The only answer the page needs is the date of birth. You can skip anything else, or choose unsure.
    • It helps to have nearby: doctor, therapy or school papers, and the dates you have called or written to Lucas DD.
    • The page does not send anything. At the end you get a message you send yourself, if you want to.

    Papers that help, for age 3 and up

    Lucas DD has not published its own list. These are the papers other Ohio boards ask for, and what the rule points to. Ask Intake which ones they need.

    • Proof that the person lives in Lucas County (a lease, a utility bill, an ID with the address)
    • Medical records that state the current diagnoses and the testing done
    • A psychological evaluation, if one exists (other Ohio boards require one with IQ and adaptive scores for an intellectual disability diagnosis)
    • School records: Evaluation Team Report or Multi-Factored Evaluation, IEP, 504 plan, if the person is or was in school
    • Guardianship papers (the probate court's appointment), only if a court has appointed a guardian
    • Signed releases of information so the board can request records for you
    • The eligibility letter from another Ohio county board, only if the person was found eligible there before moving

    What the questions mean

    First name (optional)
    Only if you want it on the letter or message. You can leave it blank.
    Date of birth
    The person's birthday. The page uses their age to find the right office. This is the one answer the page needs.
    Do they live in Lucas County?
    Lucas DD serves people who live in Lucas County. If the person lives in another county, the page points you to that county's board.
    Do they live with family?
    Does the person live at home with family? This matters for some help, like Family Support Services.
    Your name (optional)
    Your name, if you want it on what you send.
    Your relationship to the person
    How you know the person: parent, guardian, the person themselves, or someone else.
    Your phone (optional)
    So Lucas DD can call you back. Nothing is sent from this page.
    Your email (optional)
    So Lucas DD can write to you. Nothing is sent from this page.
    Diagnoses, separated by commas (optional)
    Any condition a doctor or specialist has named. Use their words. You can leave it blank.
    Developmental delay areas an evaluator has named
    For a child under 6 only. The areas where an evaluator said the child is behind. Use the evaluator's words.
    Did the disability begin before age 22?
    Did it start before age 22? The state says a formal diagnosis before 22 is not needed. Records and history can show it.
    Is it expected to continue indefinitely?
    Is it expected to last, with no end date? That is what the rule means by indefinitely.
    Life areas (age six and up)
    For each everyday area, how much does the disability limit the person? Substantial means a big limit. Some means a smaller one (our plain words). If you do not know, choose unsure. Lucas DD's own assessment decides.
    • self-care: Taking care of yourself day to day.
    • receptive and expressive language: Understanding what others say, and saying what you mean.
    • learning: Learning new things.
    • mobility: Moving around.
    • self-direction: Making your own choices and plans.
    • capacity for independent living: Being able to live on your own.
    • economic self-sufficiency: Earning a living. This one counts only at age 16 and over.
    These are our plain words for the rule's terms. Lucas DD's assessment uses the rule's meaning.
    Records
    For each paper: you have it, you need help getting it, you do not know, or it does not apply. The page makes a list of what is still needed.
    Date you asked Centralized Intake for an eligibility determination (if you have)
    The day you first asked Intake, if you have. Lucas DD has 45 days to decide. We count them from this day, or from the day it had every paper it asked for, whichever is later (our reading of the rule).
    Date the board had every record it asked for (if known)
    The day Lucas DD told you it had every paper it asked for, if you know it.
    Date on a denial notice (only if you received one)
    Only if a county board sent you a letter turning down the request. You have 90 days from that letter to appeal.
    Already found eligible by an Ohio county board?
    Has a county board in Ohio already said yes to this person?
    Enrolled on a Medicaid waiver?
    Does the person already get help through a Medicaid waiver? A waiver pays for support at home and in the community.
    As of date
    Leave it blank to use today. Change it only to see the dates as of another day.

    What you get

    Your front door
    Where to go first: Lucas DD Intake, Help Me Grow for a child under 3, or another county's board.
    Screen
    A first look at your answers next to the rule. It says likely meets, unclear or unlikely. It is not a decision. Only Lucas DD decides, and all three end with the number to call.
    Records
    The papers to gather, and which ones you still need.
    Dates
    Dates the rules set, with the rule next to each one. A date marked UNVERIFIED is not confirmed, so ask about it.
    Your packet
    A message you can read on the phone or send. You send it yourself.

    What to do next

    1. Call Centralized Intake at 419-381-8320, or email Intake@lucasdd.org. Use your packet.
    2. For a child under 3, call Help Me Grow at 419-665-3322.
    3. Save or copy your results before you close the page.

    Family Support Services: help paying for what the family needs

    Use this page to ask Lucas DD to help pay for things the family needs because of the disability, like respite care, equipment or changes to the home. It is for a person who lives at home with family. It writes a request you give to your Lucas DD staff person.

    Open this page

    Before you start

    • It helps to have nearby: price quotes, bills or receipts, and a note from a doctor, therapist or teacher if you have one.
    • Ask before you spend. The law says to apply before you buy.
    • You can add more than one request. You can skip anything, or choose unsure.

    Papers that help

    • An itemized quote, estimate or invoice for the item or service, dated before you buy
    • Receipts or paid statements, after approval, for what you are asking the board to reimburse
    • A note from the doctor, therapist or teacher saying why the item or service is needed because of the disability
    • Insurance's answer (a denial, an explanation of benefits, or a note that it does not cover this), or a line saying why insurance does not apply
    • Income documents, if the board's policy asks for them (the board decides; ask your staff person)
    • Who will provide the respite care, where, and for how many hours or days

    The board's areas: respite care, counseling, training and education, special diets, adaptive equipment, home modifications, other (only if it is in the service plan).

    This program is for families who live in Lucas County.

    Family Support Services are for a person who lives at home with family.

    A place where the person lives that is paid under a contract with the state or a county board. If so, this program cannot pay.

    Your name, if you want it on what you send.

    How you know the person: parent, guardian, the person themselves, or someone else.

    So Lucas DD can call you back. Nothing is sent from this page.
    Your email
    So Lucas DD can write to you. Nothing is sent from this page.
    Has Lucas DD found the person eligible for its services?
    If not yet, start with the front door page.
    Your Lucas DD staff person's name, if you know it
    Your staff person helps fill out and turn in the forms. If you do not know who that is, Intake can tell you: 419-381-8320.
    Their role
    Service and Support Specialist, or Early Intervention Developmental Specialist.
    Is the person enrolled on a Medicaid waiver?
    Say if the person is on a waiver. One old source said a waiver ends Family Support Services. That is not confirmed, so ask your staff person.
    Date you gave the request to your staff person (if you have)
    The day you gave your request to your staff person, if you have.
    Date the board had your statement of expenses and the evidence it asked for (if known)
    The day Lucas DD had your list of costs and the proof it asked for. By law, the board pays within 45 days after that.

    What you need, in plain words. For example: weekend respite care through December.

    Did you already pay? The law says to ask before you spend, so say either way.

    Say if insurance or another program was asked to pay first.

    When you need it, if there is a date.
    Is it in the person's service plan?
    Is it written in the person's service plan? This matters if you picked other.
    Evidence for this request:
    Papers that show the cost and the need. Mark each one: have it, need help getting it, unsure, or does not apply.

    A first look at whether the program fits: ready to ask, ask first, or check with Intake. It is not a decision.
    Your requests
    Each request, its area, and the papers that go with it.

    Waiting list: ask for more help at home

    Open this page

    Before you start

    • It helps to have nearby: your Lucas DD staff person's name, and the dates you asked or talked with Lucas DD.
    • Give two ways to reach you, like an email and a mailing address, and keep them up to date.
    • The page writes a letter you send yourself. You can skip anything, or choose unsure.

    So Lucas DD can call you back. Nothing is sent from this page.

    A second way to reach you. The rule lets a board take a name off the list if you do not answer when it tries two different ways, like email and a tracked letter. Keep your email and address up to date.

    The waiting list is for people Lucas DD serves. If not yet, start with the front door page.

    The SSA's name, if you know it
    Your staff person's name, if you know it.

    If yes, you can still ask about a need the waiver does not meet.

    The unmet need, in your own words
    What help is missing? Say it in your own words. No special words needed.
    Services that would meet it, separated by commas (optional)
    Services you think would help, if you know. For example: respite, personal care hours, adult day support.
    Other help already tried or in place (optional)
    Other help you have tried, or already have.

    Mark yes if it fits. Mark unsure if you cannot say. Leave the rest. Lucas DD's check decides.

    3. Save or copy your results before you close the page.

    Appeal or complaint: when you disagree with Lucas DD

    Use this page if Lucas DD turned down a request for a service, cut it, paused it or stopped it, or made an eligibility decision you disagree with. You can also use it for a complaint about how things are done. It works out your time limits and writes a letter you send yourself.

    • You have 90 days from the letter to appeal. To keep a service while you appeal, act before the day the change starts.
    • You can have someone help you at any time, like a friend or an advocacy group. You can skip anything, or choose unsure.

    Words you might see

    Lucas DD
    The Lucas County Board of Developmental Disabilities. It is the county agency that decides and provides services.
    Centralized Intake
    Lucas DD's front desk for new requests, for age 3 and up. Phone 419-381-8320.
    Eligible, eligibility
    Whether a person can get Lucas DD services. Lucas DD staff decide this. The pages never do.
    Determination
    Lucas DD's decision about eligibility.
    Developmental disability
    In short: a disability that started before age 22, is expected to last, and makes everyday life harder in several areas. The rule has more parts. Lucas DD staff apply it.
    Early Intervention, Help Me Grow
    Help for children from birth to age 3 with a delay in one or more areas of development. It is free, and family income does not matter. Help Me Grow takes the referrals in Lucas County: 419-665-3322.
    Service and Support Specialist (SSA)
    Your Lucas DD staff person. They help with forms and requests.
    Medicaid waiver
    A program that pays for support at home and in the community. Ohio has three: Individual Options, Level One, and Self-Empowered Life Funding.
    Waiting list
    Lucas DD's list of people waiting for a waiver.
    Family Support Services (FSS)
    County money that helps a family pay for things needed because of the disability, for a person who lives at home with family.
    Respite care
    Short-term care for the person, so the family can keep going or handle a planned or urgent need. It can be in or out of the home.
    Release of information
    A form you sign so Lucas DD can ask others for records for you.
    Appeal
    Asking for a decision to be looked at again, in writing, within 90 days of the letter.
    Complaint
    Telling Lucas DD about a problem with a program, a service, a policy or how things are done.
    Advocate
    Someone who helps you, like a friend, a family member or an advocacy group. The rule says an advocate may help at any time.
    Ombudsman
    A neutral helper at Lucas DD when a family has a problem with the board. Phone 419-381-5262.
    UNVERIFIED
    We could not confirm this on the official page. Ask before you rely on it.
